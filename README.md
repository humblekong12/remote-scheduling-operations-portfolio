# Remote Administrative & Scheduling Support Simulation

This repository contains documentation, workflows, and templates for a mini-project designed to demonstrate core competencies required for remote administrative, scheduling, and operations roles. 

These simulations mimic real-world applicant tracking systems (ATS), customer relationship management (CRM) workflows, and calendar logistics.

---

## Project 1: Remote Interview Scheduling Simulation
**Objective:** Coordinate complex calendars for multiple stakeholders, eliminate scheduling conflicts, and establish clear candidate communication templates.

### 1. Calendar Management Strategy
* **Stakeholders Managed:** 2 Recruiters (Recruiter A - Blue, Recruiter B - Green) with fluctuating daily availability.
* **Conflict Resolution:** Cross-referenced recruiter calendars to systematically isolate 30-minute availability blocks, ensuring zero overlapping bookings or double-bookings.
* **Timezone Standard:** All mock slots were synchronized to US Eastern Standard Time (EST) to simulate international team alignment.
